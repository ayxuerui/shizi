On page load, this tries to play a short tone with no user gesture. On iOS Safari this should fail. Check the log for autoplay result: blocked — that confirms the constraint the app design accounts for (a first-gesture unlock screen).

2. Audio unlock via tap (expected to SUCCEED)

CONFIRMED FINDING (2026-08-17, iPad Air Safari): buttons 1 & 2 (WebAudio) are silent on their own, even though state reports "running". Button 3 (<audio> element) is audible — and once it's been played once, buttons 1 & 2 become audible too. Conclusion: AudioContext.resume() alone doesn't fully open the native audio session on this device; an HTMLAudioElement play does, and after that WebAudio works too (same underlying session). See design.md "Audio unlock requires an HTMLMediaElement" — this is now a load-bearing requirement for task 8.3, not a spike curiosity.
